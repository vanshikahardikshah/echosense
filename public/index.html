EchoSense

Your phone's speaker and microphone, working as a sonar. It notices movement, breathing and falls without a camera.

  1. Lay the phone flat on a table, screen up, with nothing covering the bottom edge. Turn the volume all the way up and silent mode off.
  2. Tap Start sensing and allow the microphone and motion access.
  3. Keep the room still for about five seconds while it learns the echoes.

Sound is analysed on this phone and is never recorded or sent anywhere. The sonar uses tones above 18 kHz, which most adults cannot hear; some pets can.
